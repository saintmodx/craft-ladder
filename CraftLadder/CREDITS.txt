Craft Ladder: data sources and credits

Craft Ladder is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 (see LICENSE).

The data it ships (Data/*.lua) is built from:

- World of Warcraft: Forever's client tables (Blizzard Entertainment), read through wago.tools: recipes, colours,
  reagents, tools, items and vendor prices.
- CMaNGOS classic-db (GPL-3.0, https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db): trainers and their fees, ranks, fishing,
  what gathering and chests give, what disenchanting gives.
- Questie and QuestieDB (GPL-3.0, https://github.com/Questie): who sells a recipe, quests, NPCs and where they stand.
- AllTheThings (MIT, https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings): Merchant's Favor prices, where recipes are sold,
  rewarded and dropped, and which aren't out yet. Its notice is below.
- GatherMate2_Data (kagaro, Xinhuan, Spinx, Marrtin): where nodes, herbs, fishing pools and chests have been seen.
- What trainers and quartermasters showed in the author's game.

In game, Craft Ladder reads prices from AuctionDB and Auctionator when they're installed; nothing of theirs is
shipped.
